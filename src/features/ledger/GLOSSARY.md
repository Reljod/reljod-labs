# Ledger

The cat banker mascot who answers a visitor's question about Reljod by taking the site apart and rebuilding it into an answer.

## Language

**Ledger**:
The mascot: a ginger tabby in a navy suit who answers questions about Reljod using only what the site says.
_Avoid_: Bot, assistant, agent, chatbot

**Ask session**:
One visitor's conversation with Ledger, kept at its own address so the home page is never changed. Asking from the home page opens one in place: the address changes, the page never reloads. Holds every Build in order.
_Avoid_: Chat, thread, conversation

**Build**:
Ledger answering one question in an Ask session: taking the page apart, pushing the right Content blocks onto the Stack, kicking the rest of the page away and writing the Stack up.
_Avoid_: Run, response, turn

**Build plan**:
The ordered steps Ledger follows for one Build, and the short answer he says in chat. Names Content blocks and what happens to each, never where things are on screen.
_Avoid_: Script, choreography, actions

**Content block**:
A piece of what the site says that Ledger cuts out during a Build, as large as a whole job or as small as one sentence; he decides its size. Its words are always the site's own.
_Avoid_: Card, chunk, section, widget

**Stack**:
Where Ledger piles Content blocks during a Build, above the page he takes them from, in answer order.
_Avoid_: Board, canvas, workspace

**Write-up**:
The closing beat of a Build and what it leaves: sentences Ledger writes from the Stack, each citing the Content blocks it rests on. A sentence that cites no kept block is dropped.
_Avoid_: Reform, summary, response

**Source block**:
A Content block once the Write-up cites it: numbered, floating beside the sentences, opened or closed by the visitor.
_Avoid_: Citation card, footnote, reference

**Dock**:
Where the chat with Ledger lives during an Ask session, beside the page (below it on a phone), with his terminal underneath. Ledger pushes the home page's chat box into it.
_Avoid_: Sidebar, panel, drawer
