# Helio (autonomous global product shoot & campaign studio): agent notes

Read this before you change `src/features/helio/`, `/demos/helio`, `/api/helio/*`, `public/helio/` or `e2e/helio.spec.ts`. The rules for every demo are in the root `AGENTS.md`; the layers are in `docs/engineering/architecture.md`.

- A live demo at `/demos/helio` in four tabs (Studio, Placements, Flash vs 2.7, Under the hood; `?tab=`): turns one product photo into a 3-market global launch campaign using November 2026 **Microsoft MAI Image 2.7 / 2.7-Flash** (native transparent RGBA plates via `providerOptions.azure.background: "transparent"` and multi-reference reasoning-based scene transplanting/editing) paired with **Google Gemini 4 Neon** (configurable-thinking Executive Creative Director and side-by-side Vision Brand-Integrity Auditor).
- Layers: `server/service.ts` (`HelioService`: `extract`, `shoot`, `edit`, `audit`, `compare`) over the ports in `server/ports.ts` (`Studio` for the paid models, `Products` for the hero plate, `HelioRate`, `now`), wired in `server/container.ts`. `server/api.ts` is the route stack (`sameOrigin`, `withAccess`, `withVisitor`, `withServices(helio)`); Helio saves nothing, so there is no Owner. Adapters: `server/studio.ts` (`modelStudio`, `stubStudio`, `productFiles`), `server/rate.ts` (`pgHelioRate`).
- Endpoints, all `POST`, gated in `src/proxy.ts` and again by the stack, bodies up to 4.5 MB (`HELIO_BODY_LIMIT`, Vercel's cap):
  - `/api/helio/extract`: an RGBA plate (`image` × 1), JSON.
  - `/api/helio/shoot`: Neon directs, then MAI shoots each market in parallel (`director` × 1 and `image` × markets), NDJSON.
  - `/api/helio/edit`: a surgical edit (`image` × 1), JSON.
  - `/api/helio/audit`: a brand-integrity audit (`director` × 1), JSON.
  - `/api/helio/compare`: Flash against Flagship (`image` × 2), NDJSON.
- The service checks every image before the caps: magic bytes and the per-image cap (`decodeImageDataUrl`, a 400 `InvalidError`), the combined cap (`checkCombinedImageBytes`, a 413 `TooLargeError`), an unknown market or Sample (400). Uploaded images are downscaled in the browser first (`components/api.ts`) so multi-image payloads stay under the body cap.
- Caps: `LIMITS` in `domain/limits.ts`, per bucket (`image`, `director`): a visitor's hour and everyone's day, in units (model calls), over `helio.rate_hits` (`bucket`, `units`). Not the shared `pgRateLimiter`, which takes one kind per call: a Shoot spends from both buckets, so `pgHelioRate.take(visitor, { director, image })` locks every bucket in a fixed order, counts them in one query and inserts every hit in the same transaction, or refuses (429) having recorded nothing. Taken before the first model call; the e2e visitor is never limited.
- Errors: a refusal (any platform `AppError`) reaches the browser in its own words; anything else becomes a bare 500 from the pipeline, or, inside a stream, a generic line (`publicMessage` in the service) with the cause logged.
- Pure-code pixel telemetry (`domain/telemetry.ts`, `analyzeRgbaPixels`) measures RGBA alpha coverage, dominant hex swatches, 16-bin luminance histograms, contrast and Sobel sharpness directly in the browser Canvas; Neon provides vision scores and spatial callout pins.
- Migration: `supabase/migrations/20261121150000_helio.sql` (`helio.rate_hits` with its `(visitor_hash, bucket, created_at)` and `(bucket, created_at)` indexes; holds no Owner data).
- Env: `HELIO_AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY` (falls back to `DOCUMENT_COMPLIANCE_AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY`), `HELIO_IMAGE_MODEL` (default `microsoft/mai-image-2.7-flash`), `HELIO_FLAGSHIP_IMAGE_MODEL` (default `microsoft/mai-image-2.7`), `HELIO_DIRECTOR_MODEL` (default `google/gemini-4-neon`); the shared `DATABASE_URL` pool (`db()`), `RATE_LIMIT_SALT` and `E2E_BYPASS_TOKEN`.
- Stubbing: an e2e request (the e2e token) without `x-e2e-live: 1` gets the container's `stubStudio`: canned direction and audits, and the product plate (or the frame being edited) handed back as each image. Gates, caps and image checks stay real.
- Tests: `server/service.test.ts` (a Shoot takes both buckets in one take before any model call; a refusal reaches no model; a lying image is refused before the cap; an internal error never reaches a stream line), `server/rate.test.ts` (the hour and day refusals), `domain/helio.test.ts` (magic bytes, the combined cap, the market cap, the Sample plates are real RGBA PNGs). Specimen plates live in `public/helio/samples/` (`scripts/generate-helio-samples.mts`). `e2e/helio.spec.ts` runs stubbed by default.
