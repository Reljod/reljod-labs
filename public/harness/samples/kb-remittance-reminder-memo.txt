HARBOUR BANK & TRUST: PENSIONS OPERATIONS MEMO (SPECIMEN)
Date: 12 January 2024
To: Pensions Operations Desk
Subject: Reminder to employers: monthly remittance

This memo restates the monthly remittance rules for the Desk's employer calls. It is illustrative, not legal advice.

Contributions for a month of service must reach the plan by close of business on the 15th day of the following month (National Pensions Act s.48(2)-(3)). Contributions for December are due by 15 January.

The contribution is 10% of each employee's earnings: normally 5% from the employer and 5% deducted from the employee. Mandatory contributions apply to earnings up to the Year's Maximum Pensionable Earnings of CI$87,000 a year. Earnings above that are treated as additional voluntary contributions.

A contribution received after the 15th is delinquent from the next day. The employer owes interest at the Cayman prime rate plus five percentage points, calculated daily (s.50). The Desk contacts the employer at once and records each effort. A contribution still unpaid on the 15th of the following month is reported to the Director of Labour and Pensions by the 20th.

Please give employers the Monthly Contribution Schedule (HBT-PEN-01) when they call.
