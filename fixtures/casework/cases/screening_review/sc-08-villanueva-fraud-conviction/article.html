Ex-coop treasurer convicted of P12-M estafa

By Rowena Santiago

PASIG CITY - A Regional Trial Court here on Wednesday found the former treasurer of a workers' cooperative guilty of estafa over the disappearance of nearly P12 million in members' savings.

Ma. Teresa D. Villanueva, 42, a resident of Pasig City, was treasurer of the Kalayaan Workers Multi-Purpose Cooperative from 2011 until 2019, when an audit found that loan repayments and deposits had been diverted to accounts she controlled.

The court sentenced Villanueva, known to members as "Tess", to four to six years' imprisonment and ordered her to repay P11.8 million to the cooperative. Her counsel said they would apply for probation.

The cooperative's chairman said members were relieved by the verdict and hoped to recover their savings. About 600 members, most of them factory and transport workers, had deposits with the cooperative.

Villanueva did not speak to reporters as she left the courtroom.
