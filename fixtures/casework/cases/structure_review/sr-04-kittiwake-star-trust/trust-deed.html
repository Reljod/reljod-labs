THE KITTIWAKE STAR TRUST

DECLARATION OF TRUST
establishing a special trust under Part VIII of the Trusts Act (As Revised) of the Cayman Islands

THIS DEED is made on 14 April 2022

BETWEEN:

(1)   ADAEZE OKONKWO-BELL of Flat 6, 21 Cadogan Gardens, London SW3 2RW, United Kingdom (the "Settlor");

(2)   BLUEWATER BIGHT TRUST COMPANY LTD., a Cayman Islands company holding an unrestricted trust licence (licence no. T-0918) under the Banks and Trust Companies Act (As Revised), of 2nd Floor, Frigate House, 54 Elgin Avenue, George Town, Grand Cayman (the "Trustee"); and

(3)   CHINEDU ABARA-WHYTE of 9 Marlborough Place, London NW8 0PS, United Kingdom (the "Enforcer").

RECITALS

(A)   The Settlor has transferred to the Trustee the property described in the Schedule, being the entire issued share capital of Kittiwake Marine Holdings Ltd. (the "Company"), which owns the Okonkwo-Bell family's shipping and ship-management business.

(B)   The parties intend that the trusts declared by this Deed shall be special trusts to which Part VIII of the Trusts Act (As Revised) applies (a "STAR trust"), and that the Trustee shall hold the Trust Fund for the Purposes and the Beneficiaries set out below.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows:

1.  DEFINITIONS

1.1  In this Deed:

(a)   "Beneficiaries" means the descendants of the Settlor;

(b)   "Enforcer" means Chinedu Abara-Whyte or any successor appointed under clause 6;

(c)   "Purposes" means the purposes set out in clause 3;

(d)   "Trust Fund" means the property in the Schedule and all property later added to or representing it.

1.2  There is no Protector of this trust. No person other than the Enforcer holds any power of consent or veto over the Trustee.

2.  NAME AND DECLARATION

The trust shall be known as The Kittiwake STAR Trust. The Trustee declares that it holds the Trust Fund upon the special trusts set out in this Deed, which are STAR trusts within Part VIII of the Trusts Act (As Revised).

3.  PURPOSES AND BENEFICIARIES

3.1  The Trustee shall hold the Trust Fund for the following Purposes:

(a)   to retain the shares of the Company as a long-term holding, so that the family shipping business continues under unified ownership;

(b)   to exercise the voting rights attached to those shares so as to secure competent and independent management of the Company; and

(c)   subject to (a) and (b), to pay or apply the net income of the Trust Fund for the benefit of such of the Beneficiaries as the Trustee thinks fit.

3.2  The Trustee shall not sell the shares of the Company except with the prior written approval of the Enforcer.

4.  NO STANDING OF BENEFICIARIES

No Beneficiary shall, by virtue of being a Beneficiary, have standing to enforce the trusts, nor any enforceable right against the Trustee or the Enforcer, nor any interest in the Trust Fund. The Enforcer alone has standing to enforce the trusts.

5.  THE ENFORCER

5.1  The Enforcer has the rights and duties of an enforcer under Part VIII of the Trusts Act (As Revised), including the right to receive accounts and information from the Trustee and to apply to the Grand Court for directions.

5.2  The Enforcer may by written notice remove the Trustee and appoint a successor Trustee holding a trust licence in the Cayman Islands.

5.3  The Enforcer's approval is required for any addition to the Purposes, any sale or charge of the shares of the Company, and any distribution of capital.

5.4  The Enforcer shall act in good faith and shall not be liable for any loss unless caused by his own fraud or wilful default.

6.  SUCCESSION OF ENFORCERS

6.1  The Enforcer may at any time by deed nominate a successor to take office on his death, incapacity or retirement.

6.2  If at any time there is no Enforcer, the Trustee shall within thirty days appoint a successor, failing which it shall apply to the Grand Court for an appointment.

7.  MANAGEMENT OF THE COMPANY

The Trustee shall be under no duty to interfere in or enquire into the management of the Company, and shall be entitled to rely on the accounts and information provided by its directors unless it has actual knowledge of dishonesty.

8.  THE SETTLOR

The Settlor retains no power to revoke, vary or direct the trusts. The Settlor may write to the Trustee from time to time to express non-binding wishes.

9.  DURATION

The rule against perpetuities does not apply to this trust. The trusts shall continue until the Trustee, with the written approval of the Enforcer, declares by deed that the Purposes have been fulfilled.

10.  GOVERNING LAW

This Deed and the trusts declared by it are governed by the laws of the Cayman Islands, and the Grand Court of the Cayman Islands shall have exclusive jurisdiction over them.
