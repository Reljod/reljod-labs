THE BANYAN CREST TRUST

Declaration of Trust · Cayman Islands

THIS DECLARATION OF TRUST is made on 30 August 2026 by CINNAMON BAY TRUSTEES LTD., a company incorporated in the Cayman Islands and licensed by the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority to carry on trust business (licence no. T-1044), of Unit 5, Cinnamon Bay House, 12 Smith Road, George Town, Grand Cayman (the "Trustee"), at the request of ANAND VIKRAM RAO of 41 Palace Orchards, Sadashivanagar, Bengaluru 560080, India (the "Settlor"), who joins in this Deed to signify his approval.

Recitals

(A)  The Settlor has transferred to the Trustee the sum of US$100.00 and intends to transfer further property, including the entire issued share capital of Banyan Crest Holdings Ltd., to be held on the trusts declared below.

(B)  The trust shall be known as The Banyan Crest Trust.

1. Definitions

In this Deed:

(a)  "Beneficiaries" means (i) the Settlor, Anand Vikram Rao; (ii) Lakshmi Rao, the wife of the Settlor; and (iii) the children and remoter issue of the Settlor, whether now living or born hereafter;

(b)  "Named Beneficiaries" means Anand Vikram Rao and Lakshmi Rao;

(c)  "Trust Fund" means the property referred to in Recital (A), all additions and all property representing it; and

(d)  "Trust Period" means the period of one hundred and fifty years from the date of this Deed.

2. Discretionary trusts of income and capital

2.1  During the Trust Period the Trustee may pay or apply the income and capital of the Trust Fund to or for the benefit of any one or more of the Beneficiaries in such proportions as the Trustee in its absolute discretion decides.

2.2  In exercising its discretions the Trustee shall have regard to, but shall not be bound by, any letter of wishes of the Settlor.

3. Default trust

At the end of the Trust Period the Trust Fund shall be held for such of the issue of the Settlor as are then living, per stirpes.

4. Powers of investment

The Trustee may hold the Trust Fund through Banyan Crest Holdings Ltd. or any other underlying company and shall not be bound to interfere in its management.

5. Addition and removal of Beneficiaries

The Trustee may, with the written consent of the Settlor during his lifetime, add any person or charity as a Beneficiary, or exclude any person from the class of Beneficiaries.

6. No protector

No protector is appointed to this trust. The powers that would otherwise be held by a protector are reserved to the Settlor under clause 9.

7. Accounts and information

The Trustee shall keep proper accounts of the Trust Fund and shall supply annual statements to the Settlor and, on request, to any Named Beneficiary.

8. Remuneration

The Trustee shall be entitled to remuneration in accordance with its standard terms of business as agreed with the Settlor from time to time.

9. Powers reserved to the Settlor

As permitted by the Trusts Act (As Revised), the Settlor reserves to himself during his lifetime the following powers:

(a)  to appoint new or additional trustees and to remove any trustee by written notice;

(b)  to give binding directions to the Trustee as to the investment and management of the Trust Fund, including the exercise of voting rights in Banyan Crest Holdings Ltd.;

(c)  to consent to or veto any distribution of capital exceeding US$100,000; and

(d)  to change the proper law of this trust.

The reservation of these powers shall not invalidate the trusts declared by this Deed.

10. Exoneration

The Trustee shall not be liable for any loss arising from its compliance with a direction given by the Settlor under clause 9(b).

11. Proper law

This trust is governed by the laws of the Cayman Islands and the courts of the Cayman Islands shall have jurisdiction in all matters relating to it.

12. Irrevocability

This trust is irrevocable, save that the powers in clause 9 may be exercised in accordance with their terms.
