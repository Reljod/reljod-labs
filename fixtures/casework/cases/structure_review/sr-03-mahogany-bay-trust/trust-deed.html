THE MAHOGANY BAY TRUST

SETTLEMENT

THIS SETTLEMENT is made on the 22nd day of July 2019

BETWEEN:

(1)   ISABELA DUARTE KIMURA of 18 Wellesley Street East, Toronto, Ontario, Canada (the "Settlor"); and

(2)   SEAGRAPE FIDUCIARY SERVICES LTD., a company incorporated in the Cayman Islands and holding a trust licence (licence no. T-0731) issued by the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority under the Banks and Trust Companies Act (As Revised), whose registered office is at 4th Floor, Mangrove Court, 23 Dr. Roy's Drive, George Town, Grand Cayman (the "Original Trustee").

RECITALS

(A)   The Settlor wishes to make this Settlement and has paid to the Original Trustee the sum of ten United States dollars (US$10.00) to be held on the trusts declared below.

(B)   It is intended that further money, investments and other property may be paid or transferred to the Trustees, including the entire issued share capital of Mahogany Bay Holdings Ltd., a Cayman Islands exempted company.

(C)   This Settlement shall be known as THE MAHOGANY BAY TRUST.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows:

1.  DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1  In this Settlement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Beneficiaries" means the children and remoter issue of the Settlor, whether born before or after the date of this Settlement, and any person or charity added under clause 7;

"Excluded Person" means the Settlor and any person (other than a Beneficiary) who has added property to the Trust Fund;

"Protector" means Augustine Rivers-McLaughlin or such other person or persons as may for the time being hold office as Protector under clause 9;

"Trust Period" means the period ending on the day before the one hundred and fiftieth anniversary of the date of this Settlement, which is the perpetuity period applicable to it;

"Trustees" means the Original Trustee or other the trustee or trustees for the time being of this Settlement; and

"Trust Fund" means the sum referred to in Recital (A), all property later paid or transferred to and accepted by the Trustees as additions, and all property from time to time representing the same.

1.2  Headings are for convenience only. The singular includes the plural and references to a person include a body corporate or charity.

2.  DISCRETIONARY TRUSTS

2.1  The Trustees shall hold the capital and income of the Trust Fund upon trust for or for the benefit of such one or more of the Beneficiaries, at such ages or times, in such shares and in such manner as the Trustees in their absolute discretion think fit.

2.2  Any income not paid or applied within twelve months of its receipt may be accumulated and added to the capital of the Trust Fund.

3.  DEFAULT TRUSTS

Subject to clause 2, at the end of the Trust Period the Trustees shall hold the Trust Fund for such of the children of the Settlor as are then living, and if more than one in equal shares.

4.  ADMINISTRATIVE POWERS

4.1  The Trustees may invest the Trust Fund in any investments of whatever nature and wherever situated as if they were absolutely entitled to it, and may hold any part of it through one or more underlying companies.

4.2  The Trustees shall not be bound to interfere in the management of any underlying company, and may appoint any of their officers or affiliates as directors of it.

4.3  The Trustees may open and operate accounts with any bank, trust company or broker in any jurisdiction.

5.  EXCLUSION OF THE SETTLOR

No Excluded Person shall be capable of taking any benefit under this Settlement, and no power shall be exercised so as to confer a benefit on an Excluded Person.

6.  IRREVOCABILITY

This Settlement is irrevocable. The Settlor reserves no power to revoke or vary it.

7.  ADDITION AND EXCLUSION OF BENEFICIARIES

7.1  The Trustees may, with the prior written consent of the Protector, by deed add any person (other than an Excluded Person) or any charity to the class of Beneficiaries.

7.2  The Trustees may by deed exclude any person from the class of Beneficiaries, either permanently or for a stated period.

8.  APPOINTMENT AND REMOVAL OF TRUSTEES

8.1  The power of appointing new or additional Trustees shall be vested in the Protector.

8.2  The Protector may by written notice remove any Trustee, provided that a successor Trustee holding a licence under the Banks and Trust Companies Act (As Revised), or an equivalent licence in another jurisdiction, is appointed at the same time.

9.  THE PROTECTOR

9.1  The first Protector shall be Augustine Rivers-McLaughlin of 7 Seahorse Lane, Savannah, Grand Cayman.

9.2  The prior written consent of the Protector shall be required to (a) any addition of a Beneficiary; (b) any change of the proper law; and (c) any distribution of capital exceeding US$250,000 in any calendar year.

9.3  A Protector may retire by deed and may by the same or another deed appoint a successor Protector. If there is no Protector, the Trustees may appoint one.

9.4  The powers of the Protector are fiduciary. The Protector shall not be liable for any act or omission made in good faith.

10.  PROPER LAW AND FORUM

10.1  This Settlement is established under the laws of the Cayman Islands and the rights of all parties and the construction and effect of each of its provisions shall be governed by the laws of the Cayman Islands.

10.2  The courts of the Cayman Islands shall be the forum for the administration of this Settlement. The Trustees may, with the consent of the Protector, change the proper law to that of any other jurisdiction which recognises trusts.

11.  TRUSTEE REMUNERATION

The Original Trustee and any professional Trustee may charge fees for its services in accordance with its published terms of business as amended from time to time.
