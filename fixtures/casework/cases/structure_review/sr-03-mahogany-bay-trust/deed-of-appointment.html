DEED OF RETIREMENT AND APPOINTMENT OF PROTECTOR

The Mahogany Bay Trust

THIS DEED is made on 5 February 2024

BY:

(1)  AUGUSTINE RIVERS-McLAUGHLIN of 7 Seahorse Lane, Saltmeadow, Grand Meridia (the "Retiring Protector");

(2)  MALCOLM EBANKS-THORNTON of 31 Lemon Tree Bay Avenue, Corran Bay, Grand Meridia (the "New Protector"); and

(3)  SEAGRAPE FIDUCIARY SERVICES LTD. in its capacity as trustee of the Settlement referred to below (the "Trustee").

WHEREAS:

(A)  This Deed is supplemental to a Settlement dated 22 July 2019 made between Isabela Duarte Kimura as settlor and the Trustee, known as The Mahogany Bay Trust (the "Settlement").

(B)  The Retiring Protector is the present Protector of the Settlement. By clause 9.3 of the Settlement a Protector may retire by deed and may by the same deed appoint a successor Protector.

(C)  The Retiring Protector wishes to retire and to appoint the New Protector in his place, and the New Protector has agreed to act.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows:

1.   In exercise of the power in clause 9.3 of the Settlement, the Retiring Protector APPOINTS Malcolm Ebanks-Thornton to be the Protector of the Settlement with effect from the date of this Deed.

2.   The Retiring Protector RETIRES as Protector with effect from the same time and is discharged from all further powers and duties as Protector. Augustine Rivers-McLaughlin is accordingly removed from office.

3.   The New Protector accepts the office of Protector and agrees to exercise his powers in accordance with the Settlement.

4.   The Trustee acknowledges the retirement and appointment and will record them in the trust register.

5.   This Deed is governed by the laws of Meridia.

IN WITNESS whereof the parties have executed this instrument as a deed on the date written above.
