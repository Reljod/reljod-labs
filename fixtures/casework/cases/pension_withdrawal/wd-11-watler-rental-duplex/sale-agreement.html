IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. Interpretation. In this Agreement the expressions defined in the Particulars above have the meanings given there; "Completion" means completion of the sale and purchase under this Agreement; and "Business Day" means a day on which banks in Grand Meridia are open for general business.

2. Sale. The Vendor shall sell and the Purchaser shall purchase the Property at the Purchase Price on the terms of this Agreement.

3. Deposit. On signing this Agreement the Purchaser shall pay the Deposit to the Vendor's Attorneys, who shall hold it as stakeholders pending Completion. The Deposit shall be credited towards the Purchase Price on Completion.

4. Title. The Vendor is the registered proprietor of the Property under the Registered Land Act with absolute title and shall transfer it free from all charges, cautions and restrictions. Any charge registered against the Property shall be discharged by the Vendor on or before Completion out of the Purchase Price.

5. Completion. Completion shall take place on or before the Completion Date at the offices of the Vendor's Attorneys. On Completion the Purchaser shall pay the balance of the Purchase Price by bank transfer to the client account of the Vendor's Attorneys and the Vendor shall deliver a transfer of the Property in the prescribed form, duly executed, together with the keys.

6. Funding. The Purchaser shall pay the balance of the Purchase Price from his own resources, a withdrawal from his pension plan and a buy-to-let mortgage loan from Grand Anchor Bank (Meridia). This Agreement is not conditional on finance.

7. Stamp duty and costs. Stamp duty on the transfer shall be paid by the Purchaser. Each party shall bear its own legal costs. The Vendor shall pay any commission due to its real estate agent.

8. Possession. The Property is sold subject to and with the benefit of the existing residential tenancies of Unit A (monthly rent M$1,950.00) and Unit B (monthly rent M$1,850.00). On Completion the Vendor shall assign the tenancy agreements and transfer the tenants' security deposits to the Purchaser, who shall be entitled to the rents from the Completion Date.

9. Risk and insurance. The Property shall remain at the Vendor's risk until Completion. If before Completion the Property is materially damaged by fire, hurricane, flood or other insured risk, the Purchaser may rescind this Agreement by notice and the Deposit shall be returned in full without deduction.

10. Fixtures and chattels. The sale includes the fixtures and fittings and the chattels listed in the Schedule, for which no part of the Purchase Price is apportioned.

11. Default. If either party fails to complete in accordance with this Agreement, the other may serve notice requiring completion within fourteen days. If the Purchaser does not comply, the Vendor may forfeit the Deposit and resell the Property. If the Vendor does not comply, the Purchaser may rescind and recover the Deposit or seek specific performance.

12. Vendor's warranties. The Vendor warrants that (a) it has not received any notice from a public authority affecting the Property that has not been disclosed in writing to the Purchaser; (b) the buildings on the Property were completed under planning permission and a certificate of occupancy was issued for them; (c) the boundaries of the Property are as shown on the registry map and there are no disputes with neighbouring owners; and (d) electricity and piped water are connected to the Property.

13. Apportionments. Land and property charges, insurance premiums paid in advance, electricity, water and other outgoings shall be apportioned between the parties as at the Completion Date, and any balance shall be settled on Completion.
