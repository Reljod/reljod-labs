Planning points for Gull Cottage

  1. A gift of the cottage to Ms Ivy Marsh would be a potentially exempt transfer: free of inheritance tax if Mrs Marsh survives seven years, with taper relief from year three.
  2. If Mrs Marsh keeps using the cottage after the gift without paying a market rent, it would be a gift with reservation and stay in her estate.
  3. Capital gains tax may be due on the gift on the rise in value since 2012 for Mrs Marsh's original half, and since 14 March 2026 for the inherited half.
  4. Ms Ivy Marsh is expecting her first child in January 2027. A transfer into a trust for Ms Marsh and her children could be considered instead of an outright gift.
