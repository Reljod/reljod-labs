Transfer restrictions (shareholders' agreement dated 18 June 2026)

  1. Lock-up (clause 14.2). No founder may sell, pledge or otherwise transfer shares before 31 March 2027 without investor majority consent.
  2. Permitted secondary (clause 14.5). After the lock-up, each founder may sell up to 1,200,000 ordinary shares in one company-organised secondary window. At the Series C price this is M$15,000,000 for Mr Okoro. The board has pencilled in April to May 2027 for the window.
  3. Right of first refusal (clause 15). Silverpine Ventures IV LP, then the other preferred holders pro rata, may buy any shares offered at the same price within 20 business days.
  4. Co-sale (clause 16). Preferred holders may join any founder sale above 5% of that founder's holding.
  5. Drag-along (clause 18). A sale approved by the board and 60% of preferred holders binds all shareholders.
  6. Estate planning transfers (clause 14.8). Transfers to a family trust or a wholly owned holding company are permitted with notice, provided the transferee signs a deed of adherence.
