# Forms Index

Harbour Bank & Trust Pension Plan, Forms Index, edition of 1 February 2024. SPECIMEN, a fictional bank. Illustrative, not legal advice. References beginning HBT are this fictional bank's own; the other forms are the Department of Labour and Pensions (DLP) forms the plan uses.

## Member forms

| Form | Used for | Who signs | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual Transfer Request, Defined Contribution Plan | Transfer of a balance to another plan, including one outside the Islands | The member and the receiving plan's administrator | DLP, published 19 October 2023 |
| Supplemental Form, International Transfer Questionnaire | Confirms the overseas plan locks in and protects the money | The receiving plan's administrator | DLP, published 19 October 2023 |
| Property Withdrawal Application | Deposit, reduction payment or pay-off of a mortgage (Schedule form to the Act) | The member, with declarations witnessed by someone who is not an immediate family member | National Pensions Act Schedule |
| Retirement Election | Choosing an annuity or a Retirement Savings Arrangement | The member | HBT-PEN-04 |
| Death Claim Notice | Starting a claim for a deceased member | The spouse, beneficiary or personal representative | HBT-PEN-07 |
| AVC Withdrawal Request | Early access to additional voluntary contributions for medical, unemployment, housing or education | The member | HBT-PEN-05 |

## Papers that go with a property withdrawal

Proof of Caymanian status; the lender's letter showing approval in principle and the amount; for a deposit, the declaration that the applicant owns no dwelling in the Islands; for a reduction or pay-off, evidence of legal title (a land register extract issued within the last 30 calendar days); for strata fee arrears, a letter from the strata corporation; and the fee if one is charged (at most CI$50).
