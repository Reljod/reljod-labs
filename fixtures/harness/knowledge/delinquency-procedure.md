# Operations Procedure: Delinquent Contributions

Harbour Bank & Trust Pensions Operations Desk, procedure of 1 March 2024. SPECIMEN, a fictional bank. Illustrative, not legal advice: the National Pensions Act s.48 to s.50 are the authority.

## Timeline for one month of service

Service in April is used as the worked example.

- 15 May: the contribution date deadline. Contributions must reach the plan by close of business (s.48(2)-(3)).
- 16 May: the contribution is delinquent. The administrator acts at once to collect it with accrued interest and keeps a record of each effort: contacting the employer, and negotiating payment on terms the Director approves.
- 15 June: a contribution still unpaid on the 15th of the month after the deadline is reportable (s.48(5)).
- 20 June: report it in writing to the Director of Labour and Pensions, in the Director's form, by the 20th of that month (s.48(6)(a)).
- Within 60 days of notifying the Director: notify the affected employees in writing (s.48(6)(b)). Electronic notice needs the employee's written consent.

The provisions on delinquent contributions came into force on 1 July 2023.

## Interest

The employer owes interest at the current Cayman prime rate plus five percentage points, calculated daily from the day after the deadline (s.50(1)-(3)). Check the interest was calculated correctly and credited to the employee's account. The prime rate itself is not set by the Act: take it from the Desk's current rate table.

## What the Director may do

By the 20th of the month after the notification the Director must start recovery: a demand letter (payment within 14 days), a summons to explain, a delinquency collection fee, court proceedings or publication. The collection fee is the greater of 10% of the amount delinquent or CI$50 per day, from the deadline until paid (s.48(9)). An employer who fails to remit commits an offence: a fine of CI$20,000 or two years, or both (s.90(3)).

The administrator's extra reporting costs are borne by the employer, not the plan (s.48(11)). An employee may report an employer directly to the Director (s.48(15)).

## What the Desk records

For each delinquent Schedule: employer, month of service, amount due, amount received, days late, each collection effort with its date, interest owed, and the date the Director was notified. A Pipeline Run compares the Remittance with the Schedule, finds the shortfall and the days late, and drafts the employer query and the Director's report for a person to approve.
