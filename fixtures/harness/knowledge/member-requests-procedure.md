# Operations Procedure: Member Requests

Harbour Bank & Trust Pensions Operations Desk, procedure of 1 March 2024. SPECIMEN, a fictional bank. Illustrative, not legal advice: the National Pensions Act and the Department of Labour and Pensions (DLP) guidance are the authority.

## For every request

Identify the member by member number and name. Check the request is complete before work starts, and say in writing what is missing. A Pipeline Run prepares the checks and a Draft; a person approves anything that goes to the member, and an admin who did not start the Run approves a payment instruction or a rejection.

## Transfer to a plan outside the Islands

Conditions, all three (National Pensions Act s.34(3)-(5)): employment has ended; the member has been absent from the Islands two years or more, with stays totalling under three months ignored; no contributions by or for the member for two years or more.

Papers: the Individual Transfer Request signed by the member and the receiving plan's administrator; the Supplemental Form (International Transfer Questionnaire) completed and signed by the receiving administrator; the receiving plan's full prospectus or plan document; valid government photo ID. The application packet goes to the DLP for approval.

The receiving plan must lock in and fully vest the money, protect it from creditors, and bar access before age 55. The administrator must comply with a transfer direction no later than 45 days after it is delivered (s.34(6)). Deduct only actual administrative expenses that the plan provides for.

## Withdrawal on leaving Cayman

The cash refund on ceasing residence closed at the end of 2019 (s.53). Do not offer a cash refund to a member with CI$5,000 or more. Explain the three routes: transfer overseas once the conditions above are met, stay in the plan as a deferred member, or, if the balance is under CI$5,000, a single lump sum at the administrator's discretion (s.42).

## Withdrawal for property

Only a Caymanian qualifies. Caps across all the member's plans, in force from 9 December 2023 (Part 7A of the Act): CI$50,000 for a deposit or a reduction payment, and CI$100,000 to pay off a mortgage in full. A deposit withdrawal also needs the applicant to own no dwelling unit in the Islands. Withdrawals for a primary residence need the Part C declaration.

Papers: the Schedule form; evidence the applicant is Caymanian; the lender's approval-in-principle letter naming the applicant, the block and parcel number and the sources of funding; for a reduction or pay-off, a land register extract issued within the last 30 calendar days; for strata arrears, the strata corporation's letter. Declarations are witnessed by someone who is not an immediate family member.

Pay the cheque to the lender within 60 days of the application, or refuse with written reasons within 7 days (s.52B(6)-(9)). Never pay the member. The fee is at most CI$50.

After the withdrawal the member contributes an extra 3% of earnings for applications made on or after 9 December 2023, from the month after the cheque is issued, until repaid or the normal age (s.52D). Unused money goes back to the plan within 6 months, or 12 months if the dwelling is being built.

## Retirement

At the normal age the member chooses an annuity from an approved insurer or a Retirement Savings Arrangement. Tell the member the options at least 60 days before the normal retirement date, and give a retirement statement within 30 days after retirement. A balance under CI$5,000 may be paid as a single lump sum.

## Death of a member

Within 30 days of notice of the death, give the spouse, beneficiary or personal representative a statement of the benefit and their options. The spouse or civil partner at the date of death comes first; a named beneficiary is paid only where there is no spouse or civil partner and no dependent child; the estate is paid if there is neither (s.39).
