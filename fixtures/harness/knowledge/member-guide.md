# Member Guide: Retirement, Leaving Cayman and Property

Harbour Bank & Trust Pension Plan, Member Guide, edition of 1 March 2023. SPECIMEN, a fictional bank. Illustrative, not legal advice: the National Pensions Act is the authority. This edition predates the Act amendments of 9 December 2023; check the later Circulars before relying on it.

## Retirement

The normal age of pension entitlement is 65 (National Pensions Act s.3). A member who reached 60 between 1 January 2017 and 31 December 2029 may opt for 60. A member who has stopped working may choose an early pension from age 55 (s.33).

At the normal age you choose between an annuity from an insurer approved by the Department of Labour and Pensions and a Retirement Savings Arrangement (RSA) with the plan administrator. The mandatory balance is not paid as a lump sum. The administrator tells you the options at least 60 days before your normal retirement date. See the Retirement Savings Arrangement Guide for what an RSA pays each year.

Additional voluntary contributions (AVCs) may be taken as a lump sum at the normal age.

## Leaving Cayman

There are three different routes, and they are often confused.

- Cash refund on leaving: this closed at the end of 2019 (s.53). A member with CI$5,000 or more can no longer take their pension as cash on leaving the Islands.
- Transfer to a plan outside the Islands: you may ask the administrator to transfer your balance to an overseas pension plan, savings arrangement or life annuity once three things are all true: your employment has ended, you have been absent from the Islands for two years or more (stays totalling under three months are ignored), and no contributions have been made by or for you for two years or more (s.34(3)-(5)). The overseas plan must lock the money in and bar access before age 55. The application goes to the Department of Labour and Pensions for approval.
- Stay in the plan: you may simply remain a deferred member (s.34(1)(b)).

If your balance is under CI$5,000 the administrator may pay it as a single lump sum (s.42).

## Pension for property

A Caymanian may withdraw mandatory pension money for housing under Part 7A of the Act. Under the rules in force at the date of this edition, the most you may withdraw across all your plans is CI$35,000. The money is paid by cheque to your lender, never to you, within 60 days of your application. You must be buying, building or paying toward your own home.

After a withdrawal you repay by an extra contribution from your earnings until the amount is repaid or you reach the normal age. The Operations Procedure gives the current rate.

## Paying more

You may pay additional voluntary contributions at any time before normal pension entitlement. You may take them out early for medical costs not covered by insurance, temporary unemployment, housing, or education, up to four times in a calendar year.
