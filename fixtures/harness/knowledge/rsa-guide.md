# Retirement Savings Arrangement Guide

Harbour Bank & Trust Pension Plan, Retirement Savings Arrangement (RSA) Guide, effective 1 July 2026. SPECIMEN, a fictional bank. Illustrative, not legal advice: the Department of Labour and Pensions (DLP) guidance note on the RSA schedule effective 1 July 2026 is the authority.

## What an RSA is

At the normal age of pension entitlement (65) a member either buys an annuity from an approved insurer or moves the balance to an RSA with the plan administrator. The balance is not paid as a lump sum, except additional voluntary contributions, which may be.

## How much can be drawn each year

Each calendar year an RSA holder may withdraw up to an age-based percentage of the account value, or CI$16,200 if that is higher (CI$1,350 per month). The CI$16,200 floor applies from 1 July 2026; before that it was CI$15,400. Applications received from 1 January 2026 get the new floor at the next disbursement. Members whose RSA was approved before January 2017 on a more generous schedule keep it.

The age is the member's age at the start of the calendar year of payment. Examples of the percentage: age 55, 4.45%; 60, 4.73%; 65, 5.11%; 70, 5.68%; 75, 6.51%; 80, 7.66%; 85, 9.38%; 89, 11.36%.

An unused allowance cannot be carried to a later year. From age 89 the holder may take the full balance as a lump sum, with at least 60 days' written notice. The holder may end the RSA at any time and transfer the balance to an approved annuity.

## Joint and survivor pension

Every pension paid to a member with a spouse or civil partner is a joint and survivor pension at 100% (National Pensions Act s.36(1)).
