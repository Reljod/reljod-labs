# Member-Service FAQ

Harbour Bank & Trust Pension Plan, member-service FAQ, edition of 15 February 2024. SPECIMEN, a fictional bank. Illustrative, not legal advice: the National Pensions Act is the authority.

## Contributions

### When does my employer have to pay my contributions?

By close of business on the 15th of the month after the month you worked. Contributions for April are due by 15 May (National Pensions Act s.48(2)-(3)).

### How much goes in?

10% of your earnings: normally 5% from your employer and 5% deducted from your pay. Mandatory contributions apply to earnings up to CI$87,000 a year. You cannot refuse the deduction.

### Can I pay more?

Yes. Additional voluntary contributions can be paid at any time before normal pension entitlement. They can be taken out early for medical costs, temporary unemployment, housing or education, up to four times in a calendar year.

## Leaving Cayman

### I am leaving Cayman. Can I take my pension as cash?

No, not if your balance is CI$5,000 or more. The cash refund on leaving closed at the end of 2019 (s.53). You can transfer your balance to a pension plan outside the Islands once your employment has ended, you have been away two years or more, and no contributions have been made for you for two years or more (s.34(3)-(5)). Or you can leave it in the plan. If your balance is under CI$5,000 the administrator may pay it as a lump sum.

### What papers do I need for an overseas transfer?

The Individual Transfer Request signed by you and the receiving plan's administrator, the Supplemental Form completed by that administrator, the receiving plan's plan document, and valid photo ID. See the Forms Index.

## Property

### How much can I take out of my pension for a home?

A Caymanian can withdraw up to CI$50,000 for a deposit or to reduce a mortgage, and up to CI$100,000 to pay a mortgage off in full, from 9 December 2023 (Part 7A). The money goes to your lender, not to you, within 60 days of a complete application. You then pay an extra 3% of earnings until it is repaid.

## Retirement

### At what age can I retire?

The normal age of pension entitlement is 65. If you stopped working you may choose an early pension from 55. At the normal age you choose an annuity or a Retirement Savings Arrangement.
